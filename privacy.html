Privacy policy

SnookerStream Backups · 2 November 2026

This utility is used by the administrator to maintain backups in the administrator's own Google Drive account. Backup archives can include club operational records and application configuration.

Google Drive access

The administrator grants the existing backup jobs full Drive file access through Google OAuth. This permission can view, create, edit and delete all files in the authorized Google Drive account; it is not limited to backup files. The backup jobs are configured to use their backup destinations to upload, list, download and verify archives and apply existing retention rules. Full Drive access is used so the jobs can reach existing archives created by the previous backup connection.

Storage and use

Backup data is stored on the private hub, in the administrator's Google Drive account, and in administrator-controlled recovery copies. OAuth credentials are stored in private configuration. Data is used for backup, verification and recovery, not advertising or sale. Google processes data under its own applicable terms and privacy policy.

Retention and control

Backup archives are retained according to the administrator's configured backup and incident-recovery policies. The account owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account settings. Revoking access stops future authorized operations; it does not delete archives already stored. Contact the administrator who manages the backup account with privacy or deletion requests.
